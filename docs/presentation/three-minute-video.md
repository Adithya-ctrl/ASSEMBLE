## Recording checklist

- Start from Reset and confirm S0 with no downstream evidence.
- Run the real backend and production frontend; do not use mocked responses.
- Pre-fill only narration notes, not Project proof or status.
- Keep the Clinic blocked between APPLY and VERIFY long enough to see the trust boundary.
- Show exactly one actionable VERIFY control and one request.
- Capture `required 3`, `available 1`, `shortfall 2`, LEO, and the solver-run count.
- Show cost-2 training beside irrelevant/insufficient and valid cost-6 alternatives.
- Show successor identity/diff, Project source-plan identity, and Technical Inspector focus.
- Keep browser zoom and pointer movement readable; avoid hiding evidence with overlays.
- Record a clean audio take and verify captions against technical identifiers.
- End or recover with Reset so no stale Project or successor remains.

## No-overclaim guardrails

Say “deterministic fictional fixture,” “bounded disclosed catalogue,” and “independently tested locally.” Do not say production-ready, deployed, secure for real personal data, globally minimum, proven community impact, real-user validated, persistent, multi-user, authenticated, AI-powered, or complete for every community. Do not imply that CP-SAT proves facts that were never entered into the model.
